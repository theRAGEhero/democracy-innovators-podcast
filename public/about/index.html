Who We Are

The Democracy Innovators Podcast was launched by Alessandro Oppo and Carlo Michaelis.

Society is complex, and technology is evolving rapidly. We discuss how public institutions, communities, and builders can shape democratic futures together.

Mission

We share insider stories about civic tech by interviewing people working on new forms of participation.

Our goal is to highlight and add value to the projects created by civic hackers, public innovators, and democratic reformers.
